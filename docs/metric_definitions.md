# Metric Definitions and Research Notes

## Engagement rate
`(likes + comments + shares + saves) / views × 100`

## Save/share rate
`(saves + shares) / views × 100`

## Relative trend score
Within the current filtered dataset:
- Views percentile: 45%
- Engagement-rate percentile: 35%
- Save/share-rate percentile: 20%

This relative score is a prioritization aid. It does not control for follower count, account baseline, post age, paid distribution, niche, platform mechanics, or content format. Do not compare scores across unrelated samples as if they were standardized.

## Collection protocol
For each observation, record:
- Date observed / capture date in your own workflow
- Platform and public URL
- Post publication date
- Niche / audience
- Short hook observation (not a copied script)
- Format and topic
- Public metrics available at the time of capture
- A short note on why the post may have resonated

## Limitations
This MVP is a manual research tracker. It does not connect to Douyin or Xiaohongshu APIs, crawl sites, or fetch live metrics. Sample rows are synthetic.
